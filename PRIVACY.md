# Emargy Mail privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Emargy Mail is a desktop email app made by Emargy LLC (Sheridan, Wyoming, United States). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

**In short:** Emargy LLC does not collect, receive or store your email, your passwords or anything else from the app. Your mail stays on your computer and with the email providers you connect.

## What stays on your computer

- **Your mail.** The app downloads your messages, folders, contacts and attachments from your email provider. It stores them in a local database on your computer so you can read them offline.
- **Passwords and sign-in tokens.** These are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The encryption key is protected by Windows, so only your Windows account can open it.
- **Settings.** Your signatures, projects and preferences stay on your computer.

The app has no Emargy account and no Emargy server. Emargy LLC cannot see your data.

## Who the app talks to

The app connects only to these services, and only to do what you asked:

- **Your email providers.** The app connects to the mail servers you set up (IMAP to read and SMTP to send), using encrypted connections. Examples are Gmail, Outlook.com or Microsoft 365, or your own hosting.
- **Microsoft sign-in.** This is used only when you add an Outlook, Hotmail, Live or Microsoft 365 account with "Sign in with Microsoft". You sign in on Microsoft's own page in your browser. The app receives a token that it uses for mail access only.
- **DNS lookups.** When you add an account, the app looks up public DNS records to find your mail servers. When it checks a sender's DKIM signature, it looks up that domain's public key.
- **Newsletter senders, when you press Unsubscribe.** The app sends the unsubscribe request the newsletter asked for: a one-click request to the sender's own web address, a short email to its unsubscribe address, or its page opened in your browser. Nothing happens until you press the button and confirm.
- **Spelling dictionaries (Linux only).** On Windows and macOS the app uses the spell checker built into the system. On Linux, the spell checker may download its English and Arabic dictionaries once from Google's dictionary servers; no text you type is sent.
- **GitHub.** The copy downloaded from our website checks GitHub Releases for a signed update. The Microsoft Store copy does not do this; the Store updates it.

## The assistant (optional)

The assistant can translate, summarize or draft replies, and can flag important mail. It is off until you use it. It works only through the Claude or ChatGPT (Codex) program that you installed yourself and signed in to with your own subscription.

- Text is sent only when you press an assistant button.
- If you turn on important-mail alerts, the app sends the sender, subject and a short preview of recent messages.
- That text goes to Anthropic (Claude) or OpenAI (ChatGPT), under your own account and their privacy terms. Emargy LLC never receives it.

## Images and tracking

Remote images in emails are blocked by default. This stops senders from knowing when you open a message. You can load them for a message you trust.

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Your control

- To remove an account, delete it in Settings. Its local mail and saved sign-in are removed.
- To remove everything, uninstall the app.

## Children

Emargy Mail is a general productivity app. It is not directed at children.
